    It's probably not broken
    
      
    Most "slow" laptops just need a clean. Here's what's actually happening.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    
        
    Dust clogs everything
    
        
    Fans and vents fill up over time and trap heat inside. Your laptop can't breathe, so it throttles to protect itself.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Thermal paste dries out
    
        
    Usually after 3 to 5 years. Once it fails, heat can't move off the CPU properly, and everything slows down.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Throttling kills performance
    
        
    When a CPU overheats, it cuts its own speed to avoid damage, sometimes down to a small fraction of what it can do.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    You don't need a new laptop
    
        
    Most laptops people replace just need a good clean and repaste. A $72 service beats a $1,000 replacement.

    FW Tech does hardware cleaning only. No software is touched, deleted, modified, or bypassed during or after service. That includes operating systems, apps, security software, and device management tools on work or school devices.
    
          
    FW Tech does not service school or work-issued devices during an active enrollment or employment period. Your institution has an IT department for exactly this. Bypass requests of any kind are not accepted, and offering more money doesn't change the answer.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        2. No hardware upgrades+
        
    Cleaning and thermal services only. Upgrades are not offered, even if you supply the part.
    
      
    
      
    
        3. Pre-existing damage and data privacy+
        
    
          
    Every device is photographed before and after service. Damage present before drop-off is not FW Tech's responsibility. The photo record protects both sides.
    
          
    FW Tech never accesses, views, or copies any files or personal information on your device. The work is physical. Your data is yours.
    
        
    
      
    
      
    
        4. Service eligibility+
        
    Every job is researched before work begins. If a device is outside what FW Tech can confidently service, the job is declined before it starts, at no charge. A declined job doesn't mean your laptop is fine. It means FW Tech isn't the right person for it.
    
      
    
      
    
        5. Mid-service complications+
        
    If a service can't be completed safely after work starts, FW Tech stops, reassembles the device as close to its original state as possible, and gives a full refund. Payment is earned by finishing the agreed work properly.
    
      
    
      
    
        6. Cancellation policy+
