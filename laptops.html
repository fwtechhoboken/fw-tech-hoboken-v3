Not sure? Find the name in a minute

Easiest

Flip it over

Most laptops have a sticker or printed text on the bottom with the brand and model, like "HP Victus 15-fa0xxx" or "Model: A2337". A photo of that sticker works too.

Windows

Ask the laptop

  1. Press Win+R
  2. Type msinfo32 and press Enter
  3. Read "System Model"

Mac

About This Mac

  1. Click the Apple menu in the top left
  2. Choose "About This Mac"
  3. It shows the name and year, like "MacBook Air, M1, 2020"
